My Goals

Strengths

1. Working efficiently

2. Persevering once I have started something

3. I pick up new things quickly

4. Planning

Weaknesses

1. Procrastination

2. Sometimes getting distracted easily

3. Not being able to stick to my own planning

Goal 1: Strength

I am going to focus more deeply on learning Java.

I have tried learning it before, but I stopped.

This time, I will use my perseverance to keep going.

Specific/measurable: I will dedicate a certain number of hours to learning Java.

I might even build something of my own.

At the end of the year, I will look back and evaluate how this went.

Goal 2: Weakness

I am going to make a schedule that I will actually stick to.

Specific/measurable: Every week, I will list my assignments.

I will organise them in a practical way.

At the end of the year, I will look back and evaluate how this went.
